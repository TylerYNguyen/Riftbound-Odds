How to read this

Expected players is the average number of people who finish on that exact record. At or above counts everyone on that many points or more — if it's under your cut size, that points total is usually safe.

Top-cut chance is how often a player on that record finished inside the top N. Players on the same points are split by tiebreakers (opponents' match win %, then game win %, then opponents' game win %); here those are treated as a coin flip, since they depend on who you happened to play.

Day 2: turn it on for events where only players on enough points after Day 1 come back. Everyone below the line is out after Day 1 and can't make the top cut; Day 2 players keep their points and play on. If you're partway through Day 1, "Your record" shows what you need for the rest of the day, and the odds already include playing Day 2.

Mid-event mode plays out the rest of the event with your results locked in, then tries every combination of wins, losses and draws for your remaining rounds. An ID is modeled as a draw for both you and your opponent.

Assumptions: every match is a 50/50 between equal players, the odd player out gets a bye counted as a win, and pairings go high-to-high by points without rematch checks. Real events add intentional draws and drops, which usually loosen the bubble slightly.
